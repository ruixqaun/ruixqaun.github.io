Climate Design

Working with Bangkok’s heat, not against it.

Cool Roof Painting

Reflective coatings cut roof-surface temperature by 10–15°C versus standard tile.

Cross-Ventilation Layout

Operable windows and louvers on opposite or adjacent walls keep a continuous draft.

Northeast Orientation

Gentle morning light at the front; the door avoids harsh afternoon western sun.

Seasonal Wind Shifts

November–March, a cool northeast breeze enters the front door. May–October, wind shifts southwest, away from the entrance.

Site-Aware Foundations

Soft clay soil is surveyed and engineered for before ground is broken.

Thai Law & Regulations

Built inside the lines the law draws.

City Zoning — Green Zone (Kor. 4)

Minimum 40% open, unroofed area (OSR) on every plot for water absorption.

Canal Setbacks

For khlongs under 10 m wide, structures sit at least 3 m from the canal edge.

Road Setbacks

For public roads under 6 m wide, the building line stays at least 3 m from the road centre.

Legal Procedures

Blueprints and the construction permit are submitted at the Public Works Department, Klong Sam Wa District Office, before breaking ground.
